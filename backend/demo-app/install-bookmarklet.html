📌 Install Pathfinder Job Tracker

Automatically track jobs you apply to - capture job details before applying!

1Show Your Bookmarks Bar

First, make sure your browser's bookmarks bar is visible:

  • Windows/Linux: Press Ctrl + Shift + B
  • Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + B

The bookmarks bar should appear at the top of your browser, just below the address bar.

2Drag This Button to Your Bookmarks Bar

Click and drag the blue button below UP to your bookmarks bar:

📌 Save to Pathfinder 👆 Drag me!

Important: DON'T click the button! Instead, click and hold, then drag it up to your bookmarks bar.

3Use It When Job Hunting

When you find a job you want to apply for:

  • Navigate to the job posting page (LinkedIn, Indeed, company site, etc.)
  • Click the "📌 Save to Pathfinder" bookmark in your bookmarks bar
  • Enter your email when prompted
  • The job details will be saved automatically
  • Continue applying normally on the website
  • When the confirmation email arrives, it'll match with your saved job!

🎉 Bookmarklet Installed Successfully!

You're all set! Find a job posting and try clicking the bookmark.

✨ What This Does

  • Captures exact job URL - Direct link to the job posting
  • Extracts job details - Company, position, description, location
  • Saves before you apply - Tracks your intent to apply
  • Auto-matches emails - Links confirmation emails to saved jobs
  • Complete tracking - Full job details + email confirmation

🔒 Privacy & Security

The bookmarklet:

  • Only runs when you click it
  • Only sends data to your Pathfinder backend
  • Doesn't track your browsing
  • Doesn't access any personal data
  • Is completely open source
